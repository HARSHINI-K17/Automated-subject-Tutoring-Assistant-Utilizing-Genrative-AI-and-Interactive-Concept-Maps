Normalization

Medium

Academic Definition

The systematic process of organizing data in a database to reduce redundancy and improve data integrity.

Imagine organizing a messy closet. Instead of putting shoes, shirts, and books in one giant pile, you group them by category and put them on labeled shelves. This makes things easier to find, prevents duplicates, and saves space.
